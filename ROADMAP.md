# DayCrew Roadmap

The authoritative scope and exit gates are in [`docs/MVP.md`](./docs/MVP.md).

| Milestone | Outcome | Status |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | Monorepo, contracts, deterministic mock, package/app scaffolds, OSS foundation | Complete |
| M1 | Local Workspace, Teams, Team Packs, Knowledge, Member memory | Complete |
| M2 | Manager-led tasks, dependencies, delegation, handoffs, and work sessions | Complete |
| M3 | Human control, Needs You, approval lifecycle, audit, and safety limits | Complete |
| M4 | AI Engine adapters: Codex, then Claude Code, then Gemini | Codex preview; Claude Code production-ready |
| M5 | Complete local web experience: Home, Teams, Tasks, Office | Alpha implemented |
| M6 | State-derived Daily Brief and initial additional Team Packs | Daily Brief implemented; more Packs require quality review |
| Alpha launch | Installation, contributor paths, release evidence, and public launch review | In preparation |

The Product Owner controls scope, naming, priorities, and release decisions. A
milestone does not begin until the previous milestone passes its documented gate.
